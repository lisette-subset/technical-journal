
# In conversation across time with Messiah, Zettili, and Merzbacher:

Pretty excited with some books on quantum mechanics I found yesterday. 

The 2 volumes by Albert Messiah were picked up by my father in law while he lived in Baltimore for Johns Hopkins:
- Albert Messiah's Quantum Mechanics volumes
  - Typically regarded as "outdated" because of their lack of discussion on entanglement and Bells' Theorem, but still considred two classic tomes, a good peek into the fundamentals of the field. 
  - See further discussion on this [here](https://www.physicsforums.com/threads/whats-wrong-with-the-messiah-quantum-mechanics-textbook.949587/)
- Euegene Merzbacher's Quantum Mechanics
They both seem to have comprehensive table of contents. 

On the side, also learned about a comprehensive book by Zettili:
- https://www.mmmut.ac.in/News_content/02110tpnews_11232020.pdf

I am also filling in any classical/mechanical gaps with the famous University Physics by Young and Freedman, which has phenomenal visualizations along with their explanations!

I studied Math in university but have become really interested in quantum mechanics as of late while doing research in my Masters degree, I really want to understand the problems in the field at a foundational level. 

When working through problems in foundational books, I feel like I am having a conversation across time and space with the authors. It is a feeling which is hard to write about, but I feel "plucked out" of my current physical space, which is really delightful.

# Also finally figuring out my Hugo publishing scheme

I have a pretty good system for taking notes on a code editor with markdown and Dendron, with a sprinkle of emacs org mode thrown in, with an inbox system for personal, research, and work notes [shoutout to this blog post by Jethro Kuan which formalizes the inbox system idea](https://blog.jethro.dev/posts/capturing_inbox/) but am still slowly figuring out the best way to publish my knowledge. I really like Hugo as a static site generator because of its short build time, but couldn't marry the hierarchy of Hugo content folders with my Dendron repository.

I repurposed them to flag when my posts are ready to publish.
